ABOUT DRIVESCORE

How this driving safety website works

DriveScore is a student-built GPS driving behaviour monitoring website. Start a trip to measure estimated speed, distance, duration and the route on an interactive map. The trip begins with a 100-point safety score. Possible sudden braking, hard acceleration and speed above the demonstration threshold can reduce it.

01 · Start a trip

Choose real GPS tracking or the built-in simulated route, then press Start Trip.

02 · View driving events

Follow the route, observe the live metrics, and see how detected events change the score.

03 · Save your results

Press Finish Trip to store your summary and driving events in a SQLite database.

This is an educational prototype. The 80 km/h demo alert is not a statement of a legal speed limit. Browser GPS can be inaccurate. Do not operate this website while driving.
